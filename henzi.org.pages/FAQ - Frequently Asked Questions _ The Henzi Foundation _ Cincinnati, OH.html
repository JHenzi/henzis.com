Frequently Asked Questions

The Henzi Foundation is a non-profit organization based in Cincinnati, Ohio, that provides financial assistance to families who have lost a child and need help with covering the final expenses. We work directly with the funeral home, cremation service or legacy service of your choice to ensure that your loved one receives a dignified and respectful farewell.

If you're in the Greater Cincinnati area, Northern Kentucky, or Southeastern Indiana and need assistance, or if you have questions not covered here, please contact us.

How is the money paid? Or, to whom the money is paid?

The process of disbursing funds from The Frankie Fund is designed to ensure that financial support reaches the families in need efficiently and directly. When you generously contribute to The Frankie Fund, the money is channeled to assist families with their final expenses, such as funeral arrangements and cremation services.

We understand the importance of handling such funds with utmost care and transparency. Therefore, once a family is identified as needing assistance, we work closely with them when they select the funeral home, cremation service, or legacy service of their choice. The money is then directly paid to the chosen service provider, alleviating the financial burden during their difficult time.

By providing direct payments to the funeral homes or service providers, we aim to minimize any potential misuse of funds and ensure that the assistance goes directly towards the intended purpose of honoring and memorializing their lost loved ones.

Direct Payment Process

  • We work with the family's chosen service provider
  • Funds are paid directly to funeral homes or cremation services
  • No money passes through the family's hands
  • Ensures funds are used for their intended purpose

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes, your donation to The Frankie Fund is tax deductible.

The Henzi Foundation, the non-profit organization behind The Frankie Fund, has received acceptance from the IRS as a registered 501(c)3 charitable organization. This means that your generous contributions are eligible for tax deductions in accordance with IRS regulations.

When you make a donation to support our mission of assisting families in need during their time of loss, you not only provide crucial financial support to those facing the burden of funeral expenses but also gain the benefit of reducing your taxable income.

As you file your taxes, remember to keep a record of your donation to The Frankie Fund, as it will be an important supporting document for claiming your tax deduction.

What is the Frankie Fund and what does it have to do with the Henzi Foundation?

The Frankie Fund is a compassionate initiative driven by The Henzi Foundation Inc., an incorporated non-profit organization with a powerful mission. The primary purpose of The Frankie Fund is to extend a helping hand to families who have suffered the heart-wrenching loss of a child and are grappling with the overwhelming burden of funeral or cremation expenses.

The Henzi Foundation, as the supporting entity, stands firmly behind The Frankie Fund's mission, working tirelessly to make a positive impact on the lives of grieving families. As a registered 501(c)3 non-profit charity, The Henzi Foundation has gained acceptance from the IRS, affirming its status as a dedicated organization devoted to charitable endeavors.

The Frankie Fund is named after a cherished individual whose memory lives on through this benevolent initiative. The mission of The Frankie Fund aligns seamlessly with the core values of The Henzi Foundation. By channeling resources and support towards assisting families who have recently lost a child, The Frankie Fund epitomizes the compassion and empathy that The Henzi Foundation seeks to foster within communities.

How do you keep people from taking advantage of the service?

At The Frankie Fund, our primary focus is to provide compassionate and direct financial assistance to families who have suffered the loss of a child and are burdened with funeral or cremation expenses. To ensure that the funds are used solely for their intended purpose, we have established a transparent and efficient payment system.

Instead of providing direct donations to families, we work closely with reputable funeral homes, cremation services, and legacy services. Upon identifying a family in need, we collaborate with these trusted partners to facilitate direct payments on their behalf. This approach ensures that the financial assistance goes directly to cover the funeral or cremation expenses, alleviating the financial strain during this difficult time.

We believe in the power of empathy and understanding during moments of grief and do not engage in means testing or inquire about the family's financial situation. We recognize that this is a challenging and sensitive time, and our primary goal is to offer support without any additional burdens or paperwork for the family.

Protection Measures

  • Direct payments to service providers only
  • No money given directly to families
  • Partnerships with trusted funeral homes
  • Transparent financial reporting

How do you decide who qualifies for the fund?

At The Frankie Fund, our approach to deciding who qualifies for financial assistance is rooted in compassion and empathy. Our primary goal is to provide support to families who have experienced the devastating loss of a child and are facing the burden of funeral or cremation expenses. We strive to make the process as straightforward and accessible as possible while ensuring that assistance reaches those who genuinely need it.

Loss of a Child

Families must have experienced the loss of a child to be eligible for assistance from The Frankie Fund. This includes infants, children, and young adults who are 18 years old or younger.

No Means Testing

We do not engage in means testing or inquire about the family's financial situation. Instead, we rely on the assumption that the loss of a child brings with it immense emotional and financial challenges for the family.

No Discrimination

The Frankie Fund does not discriminate based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or any other factor. Our support is available to families regardless of their background or circumstances.

Collaboration with Partners

To identify families in need, we collaborate closely with reputable funeral homes, cremation services, and legacy services. These partners are often in direct contact with grieving families and can inform us about those who may benefit from financial assistance.

Do families have to pay it back?

No, families do not have to pay back any financial assistance received from The Frankie Fund. Our mission is rooted in compassion, and we understand that families facing the loss of a child are already experiencing immense emotional and financial challenges.

The financial support provided by The Frankie Fund is a gift, intended to alleviate the burden of funeral or cremation expenses during this difficult time. Families can use the funds to cover essential expenses associated with honoring and memorializing their lost loved ones without any obligation to repay.

While we do not expect families to pay back the assistance they receive, some families choose to pay it forward as a way of giving back to The Frankie Fund. This act of kindness enables them to help other families facing similar hardships in the future. However, such contributions are purely voluntary and not a requirement.

Do you pay for the whole funeral or cremation?

That is the ultimate goal. Right now, sometimes depending on available funds, just a sizeable donation would be the best we can do. Our goal is to help the neediest first, with your help we can certainly pay the complete and final expenses for a lost loved one.

We have lofty goals, and a long way to go. But, we will get there - with our resolve and the support of our donors.

What if the family has another fund set up?

Those funds could be and should be used.

Sometimes it isn't enough or money comes from people after the final expenses have been paid. If the family collects enough to pay for the funeral expenses on their own, we wouldn't get involved. But if they have money in their fund after everything is paid, that money is theirs to use however they need.

There will be expenses that they might not realize but are involved - food runs out, time off needs to be taken.

Sadly in these moments there are sometimes also mounting medical bills or unpaid obligations left behind by the child. We want to make sure every penny counts when a parent and family are suffering through this tragedy.

How much of my money goes to administrative costs?

0%. The Frankie Fund is committed to transparency. All operational expenses are covered by the Henzi family. Your donations go strictly to funeral expenses for families in need. We believe in complete transparency and accountability to our donors.
